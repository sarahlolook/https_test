Teleport to the next Room

This is the Teleport Testing, I create a grid area that works like a door , then i do a coding to connect both door. I also took some time to create a transition effect, I think it looks pretty nice.

Box Puzzle

This one took me around 3 hours to figure it out, since there is an ice Floor . The game is simple, you have to move the box to the button to trigger the door to open. There is a reset button, the one that looks like a white blob. When you step on it, it will reset the position of the box.

PaperMache

Each rat took me about 2 weeks. I crumpled sheet of used paper to build a base, then I teared newspaper, stripped it into the glue, then placed it on a base.
I painted them with acrylic. As for Rimson, I painted him in a Candy Corn pattern. I used 'Science' a lab rat from Adventure Time as a reference.
